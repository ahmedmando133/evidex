💡 Novel Emergent Scientific Hypotheses & Sweet-Spot Inflections

🎯 Evaluated Dose Spectrum & Observed Efficacy Evidence Bounded (Direct Trial Observations)
Tested Dose Spectrum: 10 to 25 mg/day @ 28 Weeks
The trial evaluated 10 mg and 25 mg daily regimens against placebo over 28 weeks/days. While both active doses demonstrated significant improvements in glycemic parameters compared to placebo, the trial was not designed to test statistical superiority between the two active doses, and did not designate either dose as statistically 'optimal' based on empirical study evidence.
🧬 Exploratory Responder Phenotype Hypothesis Exploratory Hypothesis (Aggregate Trial Limitations)
Responder Hypothesis: Elevated Baseline 24-hour mean glucose (Exploratory / Unvalidated)
Exploratory Hypothesis: Directly reported patient phenotypes require individual patient-level data (IPD) or pre-specified trial stratification. In aggregate trial results, higher baseline 24-hour mean glucose correlates with larger absolute numerical reductions, which reflects statistical regression to the mean and baseline severity rather than a verified distinct genetic or clinical phenotype.
🕸️ Cross-Biomarker Metabolic Relationship Evaluation Exploratory Hypothesis (Ecological Fallacy Protection)
Biomarker Linkage: 24-hour mean glucose ⟷ 8-iso-pgf2a (24h) (Exploratory / Unvalidated)
Exploratory Hypothesis: Cross-biomarker correlation between 24-hour mean glucose and 8-iso-pgf2a (24h) cannot be mathematically established from marginal aggregate trial summaries without patient-level covariance matrices. Reporting a fixed correlation coefficient across group means would commit the ecological fallacy.
