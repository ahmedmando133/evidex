⚖️ Multi-Drug Shared-Indication Cohort

Methodological Adaptation Strategy: Trials evaluate 2 distinct active ingredients (Metformin, Orlistat) with differing measurement endpoints and follow-up horizons. MANDOROS automatically adapted by: (1) Running direct pooling on exact parameter matches where available; (2) Constructing Clinical Outcome Domains and applying scale-free Standardized Mean Differences (Hedges' g) to bridge differing metrics (e.g. BMI vs. Body Weight vs. Waist Circumference); (3) Performing Bucher anchored indirect comparisons against common control arms with SUCRA ranking; and (4) Normalizing durations into monthly rate-of-change trajectories in predictive meta-regression. Specifically, because DPP_Extended_Cohort measured 'primary outcome' over nan nan (Reported in protocol) and DPP_Metformin_2012 measured 'weight loss >= 5%' over 2.0 years (Metformin 850mg (850.0 mg - experimental); Placebo (nan nan - placebo)), MANDOROS automatically applied Tier 2 Scale-Free Standardized Mean Difference (Hedges' g SMD) and clinical domain bridging (Other Clinical & Laboratory Endpoints) to synthesize their therapeutic impact on a unified, dimensionless scale while computing monthly rate-of-change trajectories to reconcile differing follow-up horizons.

💡 Novel Emergent Scientific Hypotheses & Sweet-Spot Inflections

🎯 Evaluated Dose Spectrum & Observed Efficacy Evidence Bounded (Direct Trial Observations)
Tested Dose Spectrum: 120 to 850 mg/day @ 2 Weeks
The trial evaluated 120 mg and 850 mg daily regimens against placebo over 2 weeks/days. While both active doses demonstrated significant improvements in glycemic parameters compared to placebo, the trial was not designed to test statistical superiority between the two active doses, and did not designate either dose as statistically 'optimal' based on empirical study evidence.
🧬 Exploratory Responder Phenotype Hypothesis Exploratory Hypothesis (Aggregate Trial Limitations)
Responder Hypothesis: Elevated Baseline bmi (Exploratory / Unvalidated)
Exploratory Hypothesis: Directly reported patient phenotypes require individual patient-level data (IPD) or pre-specified trial stratification. In aggregate trial results, higher baseline bmi correlates with larger absolute numerical reductions, which reflects statistical regression to the mean and baseline severity rather than a verified distinct genetic or clinical phenotype.
🕸️ Cross-Biomarker Metabolic Relationship Evaluation Exploratory Hypothesis (Ecological Fallacy Protection)
Biomarker Linkage: bmi ⟷ weight_loss (Exploratory / Unvalidated)
Exploratory Hypothesis: Cross-biomarker correlation between bmi and weight_loss cannot be mathematically established from marginal aggregate trial summaries without patient-level covariance matrices. Reporting a fixed correlation coefficient across group means would commit the ecological fallacy.
