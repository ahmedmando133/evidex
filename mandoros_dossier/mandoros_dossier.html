🏛️ The Four Academic & Methodological Pillars
Pillar I · Atomic Curation Stage-1 Engine

Parses unstructured RCT PDF manuscripts into a 7-sheet canonical database. Deconstructs criteria with 4 relational operators (<, ≤, >, ≥) and enforces zero-imputation guardrails with verbatim citation tags.

Pillar II · metafor-Grade Pooling Classical & REML

Executes Restricted Maximum Likelihood (REML) and DerSimonian-Laird random-effects pooling. Computes Cochran's Q, τ², Higgins' I², 95% Confidence Intervals, and 95% Prediction Intervals (PI).

Pillar III · Network Meta & SUCRA Multi-Arm NMA

Reconciles disparate active ingredients and comparator arms. Generates Monte Carlo SUCRA surface rankings, Bucher indirect comparisons, and node-splitting inconsistency audits.

Pillar IV · In-Silico Synthesis Emergent Discovery

Reconstructs missing trial cells via surrogate regression, performs dose-duration meta-regression, identifies therapeutic sweet-spots, and synthesizes de-novo virtual clinical trial protocols.

🚀 Phase-2 Multi-Trial Evidence Synthesis & In-Silico Discovery Lab
1. Missing-Data Matrix Reconstruction

When pooling heterogeneous trials, incomplete studies that omitted a specific follow-up timepoint or secondary biomarker are scientifically reconstructed using cross-study surrogate regression and longitudinal trajectory splines.

2. Dose–Duration Virtual Trial Simulator

Multivariate Weighted Least Squares (WLS) meta-regression models evaluate prospective target dosages (0–3,000 mg/day) and durations (4–156 weeks) with automated Extrapolation Risk Ratings (Interpolation vs Extrapolation).

3. SUCRA Treatment Hierarchy & Subgroups

Surface Under the Cumulative Ranking (SUCRA) scores quantify probability of superior efficacy across drug classes. Cochran's Q_between subgroup meta-analysis identifies significant interaction effects.

4. De-Novo Virtual Protocol Synthesis

Synthesizes the optimal next-generation trial protocol: pinpointing the therapeutic sweet-spot dose, duration saturation curve, super-responder phenotype, and statistical sample size for 90% power.
