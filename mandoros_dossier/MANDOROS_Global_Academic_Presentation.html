EVIDENCE BOTTLENECK

The Evidence Bottleneck in Modern Medicine

  • Manual Review Latency: Traditional Cochrane/PRISMA systematic reviews require 12 to 24 months of manual human curation.

  • LLM Hallucination Risk: Standard AI chatbots invent sample sizes, confabulate p-values, and hallucinate treatment effects when given complex RCTs.

Zero-Hallucination Deterministic Synthesis

  • Seconds, Not Years: Converts 50-page complex clinical PDFs into standardized 7-sheet relational databases in under 45 seconds.
  • Verbatim Page & Quote Coordinates: Every single number is cryptographically bound to exact manuscript text coordinates.
  • Pure metafor Biostatistics: Python-implemented Restricted Maximum Likelihood (REML) and Knapp-Hartung variance estimators.
  • Collective In-Silico Intelligence: Goes beyond meta-analysis to reconstruct unmeasured endpoints and design the next breakthrough trial.

Phase-2 Engine

Phase-2 Multi-Trial Evidence Synthesis & metafor Biostatistics

EVIDENCE POOLING
RANDOM-EFFECTS POOLING

Restricted Maximum Likelihood (REML)

Executes DerSimonian-Laird and REML models with Knapp-Hartung variance adjustment. Automatically calculates:

  • Heterogeneity Metrics: Cochran's $Q$, between-study variance $ au^2$, and Higgins' $I^2$.
  • Confidence & Prediction Intervals: 95% Confidence Interval (mean effect) AND 95% Prediction Interval (PI for future trials).
  • Small-Study Bias: Egger's linear regression test for funnel plot asymmetry and Begg's rank test.
  • Study Influence: Leave-one-out sensitivity analysis detecting outlier-driven significance.
NETWORK COMPARISONS & RANKING

Graph-Theoretic NMA & SUCRA

When pooling trials that evaluated different active ingredients or comparator doses, MANDOROS unlocks:

  • Bucher Indirect Comparisons: Indirect relative risk calculation across connected multi-trial networks.
  • SUCRA Probability Surfaces: Surface Under the Cumulative Ranking curves via Monte Carlo simulations to rank treatments from best to worst.
  • Scale-Free SMD Domain Bridging: Hedges' $g$ standardization harmonizing disparate clinical questionnaires into unified domains.
  • Subgroup Heterogeneity: Cochran's $Q_{ ext{between}}$ partitioning variance across dosage tiers and follow-up lengths.
